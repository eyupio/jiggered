    How points work

    Points are your own planning aid. Demanding activities spend points; recovery gives points back. Your morning check-in sets the day's allowance: Green keeps your full budget, while Amber and Red take off the amounts you set in Account. Poor sleep takes off its own amount too. Zero-cost activities just record what happened. You can go below zero or above your starting budget. This is a log, not a recommendation to do more.

        About spoon theory

        Christine Miserandino's spoon theory uses spoons as a metaphor for the energy available to someone living with chronic illness or disability. Everyday activities can use some of that limited supply.

        In Jiggered, one spoon represents one point. You choose your own estimates. Recovery entries reflect what you record, rather than a promise that rest restores energy. Your balance is a planning aid, without judgement.

          Your privacy

          The admin screens don't show your check-ins, episodes or profile. An admin can reset your password and sign in with it, can download a backup of the whole database, and anyone with access to the server's files can read them. Host Jiggered somewhere you trust.

          Delete my account
