A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

A little more clarity.

Is Jiggered a medical tool?

No. Jiggered is a personal log, not a medical device or a source of medical advice. It helps you record your experience and notice patterns.

Can I create an account here?

Registration is available when this server’s administrator enables it and configures email. You’ll verify your email before your account is created. Otherwise, ask the administrator for an account.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Jiggered works on phones, tablets and desktops. The signed-in app can be installed on your home screen and supports offline use after setup.

Can I take my records with me?

Yes. Your account includes export options so you can keep a copy of your personal records.
