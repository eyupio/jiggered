Think about your energy in spoons?

Spoon Theory, created by Christine Miserandino, uses spoons as a metaphor for limited daily energy. If that idea helps you, Jiggered can record your own budget and activities using personal points.

Read Christine Miserandino's original Spoon Theory essay and our guide to Spoon Theory and energy budgets. Points are personal estimates, not a medical measurement or a fixed spoon scale.

PERSONAL MEANS PERSONAL

Your story stays yours.

Separate personal logs for each account, with MIT-licensed source for people who want to run their own server. No third-party trackers or external fonts. Export your records whenever you need them.

Admins manage accounts without viewing logs in the app. Server operators can access the database and backups, so use an instance you trust.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

A little more clarity.

Is the hosted version free?

Jiggered is free to use. Check the account status above to see whether this instance accepts new registrations. Read the pricing and access details for hosted access and self-hosting.

Can I self-host the open-source version?

Yes. Jiggered is MIT licensed. Read the source and Docker setup instructions to run your own instance.

Is Jiggered a medical tool?

No. Jiggered is a personal log, not a medical device or a source of medical advice. It helps you record your experience and notice patterns.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Jiggered works on phones, tablets and desktops. The signed-in app can be installed on your home screen and supports offline use after setup.

Can I take my records with me?

Yes. Your account includes export options so you can keep a copy of your personal records.
