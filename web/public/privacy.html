Your own log. A host you trust.

Jiggered keeps personal check-ins and symptom episodes separate for each account. That separation is an app access control; it does not stop the server operator from accessing the database and backups.

Who can see what

  • You: everything you log.
  • Administrators: account names and record counts. They can reset your password and sign in with the temporary one, and download a full backup.

They can read{{end}} the database and backups in plain text. This is not end-to-end encryption.
  • The audit trail: usernames, sign-in times and IP addresses are kept for 180 days, even after you delete your account.
  • Your email address: if you register or add a recovery email, it is stored for verification and recovery, in the database and in backups.

Optional usage counts are off by default. If you allow them in Account, Jiggered stores coarse weekly task counts for up to 90 days, with no symptoms, notes, activity names, dates or IP addresses.

Accounts and administrators

Admin screens manage accounts and show account details and record counts, rather than other people's log contents. Administrators can reset passwords and download a full database backup containing everyone's records. Choose a service and administrator you trust.

Copies on your device

Offline use stores unencrypted device copies and unfinished drafts in browser storage. A fully offline device cannot learn about remote session revocation until it reconnects. Signing out clears the shared browser device copy. Read offline saving and sign-out behaviour before using a shared device.

Exports and backups

JSON downloads, CSVs, recovery files and printed summaries may contain private health information. Server backups contain account data as well as logs. Keep these copies secure and choose what to share. The export guide explains the available formats and note controls.

Deleting your account

Account deletion removes your account's records from the active server database. It does not erase the audit trail described above or copies already exported, shared or held in earlier operator backups. Download what you need first, and ask the operator about backup retention and deletion practices.

External services and links

The app uses self-hosted fonts and does not load third-party analytics trackers. An operator may configure email delivery, remote backups, or a reverse proxy with its own logs. Their providers and practices depend on the service operator. Following an external link, such as the original Spoon Theory essay, takes you to a separate website.

Ask your operator

This explanation describes the app's behaviour, not a complete operator-specific privacy policy. Before entering sensitive records, ask who runs the service, how to contact them, which providers they use, and how long logs and backups are retained.
